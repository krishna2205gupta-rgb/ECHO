# ECHO

### Your identity is what others say about you.

ECHO is our take on what social media could feel like if we removed the pressure of followers, likes, and endless scrolling.

Instead of building a profile by telling people who you are, ECHO lets your identity grow through the moments and connections you create with others.

## How ECHO Works

A simple ECHO journey looks like:

**Moment → Echo → Resonance → Room → Thread → Memory**

- **Pulse** – See a small number of meaningful moments instead of an infinite feed.
- **Echo** – React to someone with meaningful responses rather than simple likes.
- **Why You See This** – Understand why a particular moment or person reached you.
- **Orbit** – See your connections as a living network instead of a follower list.
- **Resonance** – Connections can become stronger or naturally fade over time.
- **Social Battery** – Choose whether you want to be Quiet, Balanced, or Open.
- **Rooms** – Meet people through shared context instead of popularity.
- **Threads** – Continue a connection after a Room ends.
- **Journal & Memories** – Keep moments that actually meant something to you.

## What Makes It Different?

We didn't want to make another Instagram, Snapchat, Reddit, or X clone.

ECHO doesn't revolve around:
- Followers
- Like counts
- Infinite scrolling
- Popularity
- Traditional profiles

Instead, it focuses on **context, reciprocity, connection, and memories**.

A connection can start, grow, fade, or turn into a memory. That's what makes ECHO feel more like a real relationship than a social-media statistic.

## Design

ECHO uses a dark, atmospheric interface inspired by the idea of people existing in an **Orbit** around each other.

The UI includes:
- Glowing connection lines
- Animated Orbit nodes
- Soft gradients and glass-style cards
- Smooth transitions
- Room matching animations
- Mobile-first responsive layouts
